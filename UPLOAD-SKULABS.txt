SKU LABS STOCK INTO SHOPIFY

Stock numbers are the SKU Labs Default Warehouse On Hand column.
Listing details stay as they are. These files do not contain titles, descriptions, photos, prices, weights, or dimensions, so an overwrite import will not wipe them.

Shopify only runs one product import at a time.
Products → Import
Check: Overwrite products with matching handles
Do not open the files in Excel.

On the column screen, Product Title must map to Title.
Published must map to Published on online store.
If Product Title is also set to Published on online store, uncheck Product Title.
That column can only be used once. Leaving it on Product Title renames every product to "true".
