Shopify admin → Customers → Import
File: jettribe-customers.csv
Do not open the file in Excel.

23,263 customers. One address each. Extra BigCommerce addresses are noted on the customer, and the first street address is the one imported.

Accepts Email Marketing is no. The BigCommerce column is review and abandoned-cart mail, not a newsletter opt-in. Customers who had that box checked are tagged bc-review-emails.

Dealer and distributor groups are tags: dealer-usa, dealer-distributor, distributor, industry-distribution, ebay, pending-subscriptions, subscribed, unsubscribed.

51 customers have a BigCommerce store credit balance. The amount is in the customer note and they are tagged store-credit. Shopify does not apply that balance on import.

Passwords do not transfer. After the import, select the customers and send account invites so they can set a password.
