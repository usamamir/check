Two files. Import them in this order. Do not open them in Excel.

1. Shopify admin → Products → Import
   File: jettribe-restore-images.csv
   Check Overwrite products with matching handles.
   This puts the photo back on 1,654 products that already have a picture in the old catalog.

2. Shopify admin → Products → Import
   File: jettribe-archive-no-image.csv
   Check Overwrite products with matching handles.
   This archives 1,028 products that have no photo. Archived products stay off the website.

Do not import jettribe-bc-products.csv.
