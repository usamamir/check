RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Returns Policy

Return / Exchange Website: https://jettribe.returnscenter.com

You may return most new, unused items within 30 days of delivery for a product purchase price refund. Returns can be requested with our returns website: https://jettribe.returnscenter.com. Original shipping is not refundable. Oversized items like canopies, rescue boards, and trailers are not eligible for returns. Outlet/Clearance products may have return restrictions. Please see the product description for more details.

USA Orders - Refund or Exchange requests are eligible for (1) pre-paid return shipping label due to a size or fit issue. Other return types can be mailed back to our warehouse by the customer with a mail carrier of their choice at their expense. Pre-paid labels are not provided if multiple sizes are purchased for the purpose of trying fit. A pre-paid label is not provided if a second exchange is requested. We will also provide a return shipping label if the return is a result of our error (you received an incorrect or defective item, etc.).

Exchanges (USA Only) - We will send one (1) exchange size to a USA address at no additional shipping cost, if an exchange is requested for a paid-in-full order (does not apply to event gift card or store credit orders).

International Orders - Refund or Exchange products can be mailed back to our warehouse by the customer with a mail carrier of their choice. The customer will be responsible for the shipping cost of returning the product. Exchange shipping will also be at the customer's expense.

Returns are subject to inspection upon arrival. Must be received in original condition with original packaging and labels. Products that have been used, washed, or are damaged in any other way such as pet hair or smoke exposure are not eligible for a refund. Returns with make-up or other stains that are cleanable will be charged a 15-25% restocking fee.

You should expect to receive your refund within four weeks of giving your package to the return shipper, however, in many cases you will receive a refund quicker. This time period includes the transit time for us to receive your return from the shipper (5 to 10 business days), the time it takes us to process your return once we receive it (3 to 5 business days), and the time it takes your bank to process our refund request (5 to 10 business days).

If you need to return an item, simply visit https://jettribe.returnscenter.com to begin the returns process. You will receive an approval email once the return is approved. The return will ship to the following address:

SHIPPING POLICY

While we do our best to pack and ship orders as quickly as possible. Orders placed after 6am PST (Monday - Friday) will ship next business day. Exceptions may occur if more information is needed from the customer, if customization is requested, or during high volume shipping periods such as Black Friday.

If you need quicker shipping, please call 951.246.3147 or use our Live Chat to make your shipment deadline request. We will do what we can do to expedite your order. Please note, Rush shipping is not guaranteed.

We value your business and appreciate your understanding.

FREE SHIPPING
Free Shipping for orders over $400 is Ground US Shipping, excluding Alaska & Hawaii. We reserve the right to select the ground shipping carrier. Products excluded from free shipping are PWC Trailers, Canopies, VP Racing Motorsport Containers, Buoys, and all Oversized Orders. Excluded products will be billed the shipping costs after checkout.

TRACKING
Your tracking number will be automatically emailed to you when your item ships from our warehouse. If you purchased through our web site and created a log in account, your tracking number will also be available in your order history. Please check your spam folder if your tracking email has not arrived.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPMENTS
We welcome international buyers with the fastest and most affordable shipping options! International customers outside the United States are responsible for all VAT or import taxes on your order when it is received. Please contact your local authority to learn about import taxes for your order.

FASTEST DELIVERY
Express shipping can be requested and depends on staff availability. Express must be requested before 9am Central Time to ship same day. We cannot guarantee delivery once the package has left our facility, and we do not refund shipping if the carrier does not deliver by the estimated date.

LOWEST RATES AVAILABLE
Our web site uses multiple shipping companies and automatically shows you the lowest rate available based on your order size & weight.

CURRENCY
Payment can be made with an approved credit card or PayPal. Duties, fees & import taxes: as the responsible party, we suggest that you become familiar with any duties, fees or import taxes that may apply in your country.

We offer shipping of our products to Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Cyprus, Chile, China, Costa Rica, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Hong Kong, Italy, India, Ireland, Israel, Japan, Malta, New Zealand, Netherlands, Norway, Pakistan, Poland, Portugal, Russia, Switzerland, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Taiwan, Turkey, United Kingdom (UK) and the rest of the world with approved payment.

Certain countries have size restrictions that require us to ship through alternate carriers. If your country restricts your shipment we will notify you before processing your order of any additional fees.

We can ship to virtually any address in the world. Note that there are restrictions on some products, and some products cannot be shipped to international destinations. When you place an order, we will estimate shipping costs for you based on the availability of your items and the shipping options you choose. These delivery estimates are subject to change. We cannot guarantee delivery by a certain date. Shipping rates for many items are weight-based. To reflect the policies of the shipping companies we use, all weights will be rounded up to the next full pound.
