Put photos from jettribe.com onto the Shopify products, and un-archive them.

Products → Import. Check Overwrite products with matching handles. Do not open this in Excel.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/usamamir/check/cursor/jettribe-shopify-theme-86d6/jettribe-live-images.csv

This file only sets Status to active, Published on online store to true, and the product photo from the live website. It does not change titles, prices, sizes, or inventory.

Then re-upload the theme:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/usamamir/check/cursor/jettribe-shopify-theme-86d6/jettribe-shopify-theme.zip

Do not import jettribe-archive-no-image.csv.
