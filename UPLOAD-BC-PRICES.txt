Shopify prices from the BigCommerce product sheet

Products → Import. Check Overwrite products with matching handles. Do not open in Excel.

Use this file (full catalog from your Drive sheet — 2203 products, every variant priced):

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/usamamir/check/cursor/jettribe-shopify-theme-86d6/jettribe-bc-prices.csv

Rule used (matches BigCommerce storefront):
- Sell price = Sale Price when Sale > 0, otherwise Price
- Name-plate / PVC add-ons add $40 / $25 / $60 on top of the base when the SKU has no own sale price
- Compare-at = Retail or Price when higher than sell price
- No $0.00 rows (free/warranty/promo SKUs with no BC price are omitted)

SKU-only price update (same numbers, leaner columns):

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/usamamir/check/cursor/jettribe-shopify-theme-86d6/jettribe-bc-price-update.csv
