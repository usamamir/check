Jettribe Shopify upload

Upload the theme and the product file separately. The theme zip is the only file that goes under Online Store, Themes.

1. Theme
   Upload jettribe-shopify-theme.zip in Online Store, Themes, Add theme, Upload zip file.
   Leave the zip closed. Publish the theme.
   Menu categories, including 2026 JTR Series, open from this theme and list the imported products.
   A category does not have to exist in Shopify first. Color filters are circles. Size filters are buttons.
   Payment marks match jettribe.com: PayPal, Visa, Discover, Mastercard, American Express, Klarna, and Bread.
   Turn those on in Settings, Payments (Shopify Payments, PayPal, Klarna) and install the Bread app. The theme cannot switch processors on.
   PayPal Pay Later text uses the messages account already on jettribe.com. Paste the Klarna client id in Theme settings, Payments, when Klarna sends it.
   Empty menu categories, including empty Outlet links, stay hidden. Turn them back on in Theme settings, Navigation, "Show menu categories that have no products".
   VIP Deals stays in the menu. Turn it off in Theme settings, Navigation, "Show VIP Deals in the menu" when VIP is closed.

2. Products, categories, and add-on prices — one file
   In Products, Import, choose jettribe-products.csv.
   Check "Overwrite products with matching handles".
   Upload this CSV itself. Do not open it in Excel and do not save a copy. Excel removes the size columns, and Shopify then reports "Product options input is required when updating variants."

   This one file replaces the old product file, the 20 category sheets, the all-categories file, and the add-on price file. Do not import those again.

   It has all 541 products, their photos, prices, and size options.
   Every jettribe.com category is on the product as a tag, including Ride Gear (249), Outlet (255), Casual Wear (163), Accessories (106), Wetsuits (41), and PWC Covers (62). There are 159 category tags.
   Plate, name-plate, and sock choices include None. A blank plate is +$25, a custom plate is +$60, the $40 name plate is included, and Lycra socks are +$36.99.
